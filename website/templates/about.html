Where it came from

I was DJing and promoting with Adonis Childs and Turbotic under True Family Unit Productions. Underground Existence was the solo version of that work: one corner, one night a week, featuring artists who were active in Chicago right then.

The block parties grew into underground events, and I recorded audio from as many of them as I could. That is how the archive started.

What’s in it

Beyond the live recordings, I digitized older mixtapes that mattered to me and to the scene, mostly ones that were lesser known or never digitized. Friends added their own tapes to mine and donated them to be digitized and hosted.

Mark Almaria’s first demo, SOL, came to me from Georgie Saunders (RIP). It was highly influential, I had lost track of it, and I couldn’t find it anywhere. I’m preserving it here. STARTRXX was a classic and among my first tapes. It is still very obscure, and you can hear it on this site.

Why it’s on Arweave

This is a personal archive, curated from my own vision and experience. It documents my journey and no one else’s. I built it directly on the permaweb so I would never have to archive this content again.

Every mix is stored permanently on Arweave. There are no hosting bills that carry on into the future, and no need to depend on Google, Mixcloud or any other host. The mixes are permanent. UndergroundExistence.info points to them.

This is an ongoing project. If you have a mix that belongs here, you can submit it. There is a fee to host a mix permanently.
