## Confirmed

**Who.** Bai-ee. DJ and promoter, formerly working with Adonis Childs and Turbotic under True Family Unit Productions. UE was a solo effort, grown out of that work.

**Origin.** Summer 2007. A free weekly block party, Fridays, on the corner of Western and Armitage in Chicago. Open-air format, hosting street-level DJs and crews who were actively driving Chicago's music scene at the time.

**How it became an archive.** The block parties evolved into underground events. Audio was captured from each as best they could. That became the mixtape archive. Older mixtapes that mattered to the owner and to the scene were also digitized. The owner's own tape collection was later joined by friends who donated theirs to be digitized and hosted.

**Sources.** Owner's own collection, DJs' donations, live event recordings, old files/forums/radio, influential Chicago rave-history tapes.

**Focus.** Lesser-known or never-digitized tapes.

**Rescue stories.**
- Mark Almaria's first demo, *SOL*. Copy contributed by Georgie Saunders (RIP). Highly influential; the owner had lost track of it and could not find it anywhere. Now stored permanently.
- *STARTRXX* mixtape. A classic and among the owner's first. Found, archived, still very obscure.

**Rave-history tapes, circa '99** (widely shared, influential to the owner and friends): Paul Johnson *I Need Another Plan*; Terry Mulaan *New School Fusion*; Justin Long *Live at Silly Rabbit*; Mark Farina *San Francisco Sessions*; DJ Sneak *Signature Sounds*; Derrick Carter (title not remembered).

**What makes it different.** A personal archive curated in the owner's own vision and experience, documenting the owner's journey and no one else's. Built directly on the permaweb, with the goal of never having to archive this content again. No ongoing administration or hosting costs. Each piece is stored permanently on Arweave, so third-party hosts (Google to Mixcloud) are unnecessary.

**Contributing.** Ongoing project. Submit mixes to contribute, or donate. There is a fee to host mixes permanently.

**Off-limits.** Nothing for now.
